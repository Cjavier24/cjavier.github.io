The Internet provides many services that help people find information and communicate with others. In these activities, I learned about the main categories of Internet services and practiced using different platforms for online communication. I also compared different programs to understand how each platform works and which one is most useful in different situations. For the first activity, I watched the tutorial about Internet services on TutorialsPoint. I learned about communication services, information retrieval services, web services, and the World Wide Web (WWW), the four categories of Internet services. I also learned about IP, TCP, UDP, FTP, and TFTP. This information is pretty helpful; Understanding the tools I use gives me better control, improves my problem-solving skills, and helps me work more efficiently. The second activity involved meeting online with friends. The recommended platforms were Google Meet, Zoom, and WhatsApp, and although I've used all of them, I usually don't set up the meetings. These platforms allowed me to communicate through video calls, but each one had a slightly different process. First, I created a Google Meet room and sent the link to a friend. After I accepted his request to join, we talked and used some of the available web tools, such as backgrounds and the chat. Next, I created a Zoom meeting with another friend. The process was similar, but he could join without waiting for my approval. Finally, I tested WhatsApp, which was the easiest platform because I only had to select my friend’s contact and start the call. Overall, all three platforms were useful. WhatsApp was the fastest and simplest to use, but Google Meet and Zoom are better for professional settings. Finally, I used Chrome Remote Desktop to connect to another computer remotely. My mother gave me her laptop for this activity, so I could control it from my computer. Both devices use Windows, so it wasn't difficult to set up. I connected my laptop to hers, moved the mouse, and opened applications. These kinds of applications are useful because I can open documents, check files, or run programs on my home computer while using a laptop or phone away from home. These three activities helped me understand how communication works on the Internet. Now I know the fundamental concepts, how to set up meetings, and how to connect different computers!
